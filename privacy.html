1. DATA ARCHITECTURE & OPERATIONAL PRACTICES

Vectixore LTD engineers software solutions, on-device redaction applications (Doxudel), forensic verification toolchains, and privacy-preserving utilities. Our operational architecture is grounded strictly in Data Minimization, On-Device Computing, and Zero-Knowledge design paradigms.

1.1. On-Device Redaction & File Processing (Doxudel Desktop)

When using our on-device sanitization software (Doxudel):

  • Local Execution: Document parsing (PDF, DOCX, XLSX, images), OCR processing, and Named Entity Recognition (on-device local NER) occur completely within client hardware RAM and local storage.
  • Zero Cloud Transmission: File contents, PII entities, and processed redactions are never transmitted, buffered, or stored on external Vectixore infrastructure.
  • Telemetry & Diagnostics: Strictly anonymous crash reports or diagnostic signals are collected only with explicit user opt-in and contain zero personal identifiers or document content.

1.2. Handwritten Signature Capture & Sealing

Where digital document execution services are invoked, we process vector paths (stroke data) and visual rasterizations of signatures input by the user.

  • Purpose: Processed strictly as user-supplied document payloads embedded within target PDF/binary containers.
  • Scope: Vectixore LTD does not perform behavioral graphology or unauthorized biometric pressure profiling on this input; it is cryptographically bound into the resultant payload.

1.3. Cryptographic Verification & Tamper Detection

  • Presence & Anti-Spoofing: When executing certified digital transactions, visual match feeds or device sensor checks are evaluated strictly for active authorization integrity.
  • Local Biometric Signals: When device-level biometrics (TouchID, FaceID, Windows Hello) are used to authorize document sealing, processing is handled locally by the OS Secure Element. Vectixore LTD receives only boolean success/failure cryptographic tokens, never raw biometric templates.

1.4. SHA-256 Audit Registry & Immutability

We maintain an immutable registry of mathematical SHA-256 digests (cryptographic hashes) alongside trusted UTC timestamps for sealed records.

  • Mathematical Nature: A hash digest is a one-way mathematical function; raw document contents cannot be reverse-engineered or extracted from our registry.
  • Integrity: Serves as verifiable mathematical proof of document existence and integrity at a specific historical moment.

1.5. Network, System Integrity & Client Telemetry

  • Connection Metadata: IP addresses and system timestamps are logged during server operations strictly for security monitoring, DDoS mitigation, and rate limiting.
  • Environment Attestation: To preserve security boundaries, device integrity signals are processed prior to executing cryptographic operations.
  • Diagnostics: Application performance, crash logs, and non-identifying telemetry are processed through secure telemetry infrastructure to ensure enterprise stability.

1.6. Controller & Processor Demarcation

  • Vectixore LTD as Controller: Governs licensing systems, account portals, and direct diagnostic security telemetry.
  • User as Controller / Vectixore as Technology Provider: You remain the absolute data controller for all documents, records, and personal data processed locally or sealed using our desktop software suites.

2. GLOBAL DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS & COMPLIANCE

In accordance with the UK GDPR, EU GDPR and international data privacy statutes, users maintain clear statutory rights regarding their personal records:

  • Right to Access: The right to obtain confirmation and copies of personal data maintained in active systems.
  • Right to Rectification: The right to amend inaccurate identity or administrative records.
  • Right to Erasure: The right to request data deletion, subject to overriding statutory requirements.
  • Right to Restriction & Objection: The right to halt specific automated processing or profiling.
⚠ CRITICAL EXCEPTION: CRYPTOGRAPHIC AUDIT INTEGRITY

Right to Erasure or Rectification CANNOT be applied retroactively to mathematically sealed document hashes or transaction timestamp ledgers. Modifying a sealed record invalidates cryptographic chain-of-custody. This ledger retention is mandated under the legal basis of "Establishment, Exercise, or Defense of Legal Claims" (UK GDPR Art. 17(3)(e)) and regulatory compliance obligations.
