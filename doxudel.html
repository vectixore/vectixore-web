Why Architecture Matters

The Flaw of Cloud Data Transit

Uploading sensitive files to third-party endpoints creates compliance and liability risks. Once data leaves your local machine, the chain-of-custody is broken. Furthermore, pay-as-you-go cloud redaction inflates operational costs based on volume.

The Hardware Burden of Local LLMs

Running local 70B+ LLMs solves the privacy issue but requires expensive enterprise GPU hardware. Generative models also suffer from semantic drift, offering unpredictable reliability for compliance operations.

The Doxudel Advantage

Doxudel pairs optimized, lightweight on-device NER (Named Entity Recognition) models with deterministic checksum validations. It runs entirely on standard local hardware without external API calls, providing predictable, rule-based outcomes.

Irreversible Raster Sanitization

Sensitive areas in documents are permanently flattened into sanitized rasters. This prevents reverse-extraction from hidden PDF text layers. The redaction is visually destructive and secure, leaving no residual metadata behind.
