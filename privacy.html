1. DATA ARCHITECTURE & OPERATIONAL PRACTICES

Vectixore LTD engineers software solutions, digital signature platforms, and privacy-preserving toolchains. Our operational architecture is grounded strictly in Data Minimization and Zero-Knowledge design paradigms.

1.1. Handwritten Signature Capture & Sealing

Where digital document execution services are invoked, we process vector paths (stroke data) and visual rasterizations of signatures input by the user.

  • Purpose: Processed strictly as user-supplied document payloads embedded within target PDF/binary containers.
  • Scope: Vectixore LTD does not perform behavioral graphology or unauthorized biometric pressure profiling on this input; it is cryptographically bound into the resultant payload.

1.2. Identity Verification & Cryptographic NFC Attestation

In high-assurance digital verification toolchains, the software communicates directly via device-level NFC hardware with the electronic security chip embedded within ICAO Doc 9303-compliant identity credentials (ePassports or national eID cards):

  • Machine Readable Zone (MRZ): Read visually or via OCR solely to derive entropy for cryptographic chip handshake keys (BAC/PACE protocols).
  • Chip Security Object (SOD): Validated against Country Signing Certificate Authority (CSCA) public key registries to cryptographically confirm structural non-tampering and authenticity.
  • Data Group 1 & 2 (DG1 / DG2): Processed locally or under explicit user instructions to match document integrity against cryptographic seals.

1.3. Cryptographic Verification & Tamper Detection

  • Presence & Anti-Spoofing: When executing certified digital transactions, visual match feeds or device sensor checks (depth and passive liveness signals) are evaluated to ensure active physical authorization.
  • Local Biometric Signals: When device-level biometrics (TouchID, FaceID, Biometric Prompt) are used to authorize document sealing, processing is handled locally by the OS Secure Element. Vectixore LTD receives only boolean success/failure cryptographic tokens, never raw biometric templates.

1.4. SHA-256 Audit Registry & Immutability

We maintain an immutable registry of mathematical SHA-256 digests (cryptographic hashes) alongside trusted UTC timestamps for sealed records.

  • Mathematical Nature: A hash digest is a one-way mathematical function; raw document contents cannot be reverse-engineered or extracted from our registry.
  • Integrity: Serves as verifiable mathematical proof of document existence and integrity at a specific historical moment.

1.5. Identity Binding & Multi-Factor Telemetry

Where verification services mandate persistent identity binding, verified phone numbers (via SMS OTP) or cryptographic device keys are bound to transaction IDs to prevent impersonation and fraud.

1.6. Network, System Integrity & Client Telemetry

  • Connection Metadata: IP addresses and system timestamps are logged during server operations strictly for security monitoring, DDoS mitigation, and rate limiting.
  • Environment Attestation: To preserve security boundaries, device integrity signals (detecting rooted or jailbroken operating environments) are processed prior to executing cryptographic operations.
  • Diagnostics: Application performance, crash logs, and non-identifying telemetry are processed through secure telemetry infrastructure (e.g., Firebase Crashlytics) to ensure enterprise stability.

1.7. Ephemeral Cloud Relay

When files or document envelopes are transmitted via encrypted relay channels:

  • Encryption: Data is secured using TLS 1.2+ during transit and AES-256 at rest.
  • Retention: Relayed files are stored ephemerally and automatically purged from active storage buffers after seven (7) days.

1.8. Controller & Processor Demarcation

  • Vectixore LTD as Controller: Governs identity verification transactions, licensing systems, access control, and diagnostic security telemetry.
  • User as Controller / Vectixore as Processor: You remain the primary data controller for third-party personal data, documents, and corporate records processed through our utility software.

2. GLOBAL DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS & COMPLIANCE

In accordance with the UK GDPR, EU GDPR, KVKK, and international data privacy statutes, users maintain clear statutory rights regarding their personal records:

  • Right to Access: The right to obtain confirmation and copies of personal data maintained in active systems.
  • Right to Rectification: The right to amend inaccurate identity or administrative records.
  • Right to Erasure: The right to request data deletion, subject to overriding statutory requirements.
  • Right to Restriction & Objection: The right to halt specific automated processing or profiling.
⚠ CRITICAL EXCEPTION: CRYPTOGRAPHIC AUDIT INTEGRITY

Right to Erasure or Rectification CANNOT be applied retroactively to mathematically sealed document hashes or transaction timestamp ledgers. Modifying a sealed record invalidates cryptographic chain-of-custody. This ledger retention is mandated under the legal basis of "Establishment, Exercise, or Defense of Legal Claims" (UK GDPR Art. 17(3)(e)) and regulatory compliance obligations.
