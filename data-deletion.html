Account & Data Deletion

In accordance with the UK Data Protection Act 2018, UK GDPR, and international data protection standards, you may request the permanent deletion of your licensing records, telemetry profiles, and account data associated with Vectixore LTD software tools.

* Upon submission, requests are processed within statutory 30-day deadlines.
