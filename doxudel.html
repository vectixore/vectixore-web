Why Architecture Matters

The Flaw of Cloud LLMs

Uploading sensitive files to third-party endpoints creates massive compliance and liability risks under GDPR and UK DPA. Once data leaves your local machine, the chain-of-custody is broken. Furthermore, pay-as-you-go cloud redaction quickly inflates operational costs based on volume.

The Hardware Burden of Local LLMs

Running local 70B+ LLMs solves the privacy issue but requires expensive enterprise GPU hardware. Yet, generative models still hallucinate and miss deterministic data patterns, offering unpredictable reliability for strict legal compliance.

The Doxudel Advantage

Instead of heavy LLMs or simplistic regexes, Doxudel pairs optimized, lightweight on-device NER (Named Entity Recognition) models with deterministic checksum validation. It runs entirely on standard local hardware without external API calls.

Absolute Data Sanitization

Documents never leave your workstation. Sensitive areas are permanently flattened into sanitized rasters—preventing reverse-extraction from hidden text layers. The redaction is mathematically deterministic and irreversible.
