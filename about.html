About Vectixore

Vectixore LTD is a UK software company. We design and build desktop, mobile and web applications for teams that work with data they cannot afford to mishandle.

What we believe

  • A tool should be narrow and predictable, not broad and unreliable.
  • Data belongs to the person or organisation that created it.
  • If we cannot verify a claim, we do not make it.

What we make

Doxudel — a desktop application for document anonymisation and redaction. It handles PDF (digital and scanned), Word and Excel documents, CSV and plain text, HTML/XML/JSON, and images. Detection is deterministic, and every decision is recorded in an audit trail. It works offline and is distributed through the Microsoft Store.

We announce other tools only when they are ready to ship.

Who you are working with

Vectixore is founder-led and small. Support and data-protection requests are answered by the people who build the products.

How we work

  • We publish what our software does and does not do, including its known limits.
  • Every release is verified by an automated test suite before it ships.
